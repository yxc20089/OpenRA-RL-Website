OpenRA-RL is now live! We're building a Gymnasium-style reinforcement learning environment for the OpenRA real-time strategy engine.

<!-- truncate -->

## What is OpenRA-RL?

OpenRA-RL lets you train AI agents that play full games of Red Alert through the OpenRA engine. The environment provides:

- **Rich observations**: 9-channel spatial tensor, per-unit stats, economy and military data
- **21 action types**: Move, attack, build, train, guard, transport, and more
- **Real-time bridge**: gRPC connection running at ~25 ticks/second
- **Multiple agent types**: Scripted bots, LLM agents, and RL policies
